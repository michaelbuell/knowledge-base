## Category Overview

Mental health and addiction are often connected. Substance use may be used to escape anxiety, depression, traumatic memories, stress, or emotional pain. Those concerns may also become more noticeable after substance use stops.

This category provides general information about common mental health concerns and explains when professional or crisis support may be necessary.

## Important Reminder

Healthy routines, faith, relationships, and peer support may strengthen mental health, but they do not always replace professional treatment.

Qualified help may include:

- A physician
- A licensed counselor or therapist
- An addiction treatment provider
- A psychiatrist
- A trauma specialist
- A crisis counselor
- Emergency services

See [[3. Crisis and Emergency Resources|Crisis and Emergency Resources]] when urgent support is needed.
