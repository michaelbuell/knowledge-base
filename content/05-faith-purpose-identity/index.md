## Category Overview

This category examines recovery through a Christian perspective. It focuses on developing hope, finding meaningful purpose, separating identity from past addiction, and building healthy spiritual practices.

Faith is presented as one possible part of recovery alongside counseling, medical care, treatment, peer support, and personal responsibility.

### 3. [[3. Growing Spiritually in Recovery|Growing Spiritually in Recovery]]

## Main Themes

- A person is more than the worst period of their life.
- Responsibility and forgiveness can exist together.
- Recovery can create opportunities for service and purpose.
- Spiritual growth usually develops through consistent practice.
- Faith and professional treatment do not have to compete.
