## Overview

The **Recovery & Sobriety Resource Library** is a digital knowledge base containing educational information about addiction, recovery, relapse prevention, mental health, faith, relationships, and recovery support resources.

The library is written in Markdown and organized as a collection of connected pages. Each article focuses on a specific topic while using internal links to guide readers toward related information throughout the collection.

The knowledge base currently contains more than twenty individual content pages divided among seven main categories.

## Intended Audience

The primary audience is people who are struggling with addiction, considering recovery, or currently working to maintain sobriety.

The information may also be useful for:

- Family members and friends

- Spouses and partners

- Church leaders

- Recovery mentors

- Peer-support workers

- Students researching addiction and recovery

- Individuals seeking general recovery resources


The articles use straightforward language so that the information can be understood without professional or medical training.

## Main Categories

The knowledge base is divided into the following categories:

### [[01-understanding-addiction/index|Understanding Addiction]]

This category explains addiction, the addiction cycle, the connection between trauma and substance use, common misconceptions, and why recovery is possible.

### [[02-recovery-foundations/index|Recovery Foundations]]

This category introduces personal recovery planning, healthy daily habits, accountability, and realistic goal setting.

### [[03-relapse-prevention-trigger-management/index|Relapse Prevention and Trigger Management]]

This category focuses on recognizing personal triggers, managing cravings, and responding responsibly after a relapse.

### [[04-mental-health-and-healing/index|Mental Health and Healing]]

This category discusses anxiety, depression, PTSD, trauma recovery, stress management, and recognizing when professional support may be needed.

### [[05-faith-purpose-identity/index|Faith, Purpose, and Identity]]

This category examines hope, purpose, identity beyond addiction, and Christian spiritual growth during recovery.

### [[06-relationships-and-support-systems/index|Relationships and Support Systems]]

This category covers rebuilding trust, developing healthy boundaries, choosing supportive friendships, and finding recovery community.

### [[07-recovery-resources-success-stories/index|Recovery Resources and Success Stories]]

This category provides treatment and support organizations, recommended books and digital resources, crisis contacts, and examples of long-term recovery.

## Page Structure

Most content pages use a consistent structure that includes:

- Properties
    
- A clear article title
    
- Category and navigation links
    
- An introduction to the topic
    
- Organized Markdown headings
    
- Lists or practical examples
    
- Internal wikilinks
    
- A personal reflection
    
- Related-article links
    
- APA-style references
    

This repeated structure makes the pages easier to scan and gives the knowledge base a consistent appearance.

## Navigation and Internal Links

The library uses Obsidian-style wikilinks to connect related pages.

Each article also includes a **Related Articles** section. These links allow readers to move between categories based on their needs rather than following only one fixed reading order.

Category pages provide another level of navigation by grouping articles under their main subjects.

## Metadata and Classification

Each page includes YAML metadata describing the page and its place within the knowledge base.

Metadata fields may include:

- Title
    
- Page type
    
- Category
    
- Summary
    
- Completion status
    
- Creation and update dates
    
- Topic tags
    

This metadata supports consistent organization and could also be used for filtering, searching, or automatically displaying groups of related pages.

## Sources and References

Educational information is supported by sources from government health agencies, mental health organizations, treatment resources, and established recovery programs.

Frequently used sources include:

- Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration
    
- National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism
    
- National Institute of Mental Health
    
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
    
- U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
    
- Alcoholics Anonymous
    
- Narcotics Anonymous
    
- SMART Recovery
    
- Celebrate Recovery
    
- Faces & Voices of Recovery
    

In-text citations identify the sources of specific information. Full APA-style references are included at the bottom of the relevant pages and collected on the [[References]] page.

## Images and Accessibility

Articles include images related to their subjects. Each image is accompanied by descriptive alternative text and a written image description.

These descriptions explain how the image relates to the topic and make the visual content more understandable for readers who may not be able to view the image clearly.

## Educational Scope

The knowledge base provides general educational information and links to additional support. It does not provide individualized medical diagnoses, treatment plans, medication recommendations, or emergency care.

Pages involving withdrawal, relapse, overdose, suicide risk, and other urgent concerns direct readers toward professional or emergency resources. Current crisis information is collected on [[3. Crisis and Emergency Resources|Crisis and Emergency Resources]].
