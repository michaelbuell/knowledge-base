## Category Overview

Addiction can affect families, friendships, marriages, workplaces, and other relationships. Recovery therefore involves more than stopping substance use. It may also require rebuilding trust, establishing boundaries, and developing a safer support system.

This category focuses on the relationship skills and community connections that can help support long-term recovery.

---

## Pages in This Category

### 1. [[1. Rebuilding Trust in Recovery|Rebuilding Trust in Recovery]]

Explains how honesty, apologies, patience, family healing, and consistent actions may help repair trust over time.

### 2. [[2. Healthy Boundaries and Friendships|Healthy Boundaries and Friendships]]

Discusses limits that protect recovery, communicating boundaries clearly, evaluating old friendships, and avoiding total isolation.

### 3. [[3. Finding Community in Recovery|Finding Community in Recovery]]

Introduces mutual-support groups, peer workers, church community, mentors, family support, and gradual participation in healthy relationships.

---

## Healthy Support May Include

- Family members
- Sober friends
- Counselors
- Sponsors or mentors
- Peer-support workers
- Recovery groups
- Pastors or church groups
- Treatment providers
- Veteran support
- Healthy work, school, or volunteer communities

No one person needs to provide every kind of support.
