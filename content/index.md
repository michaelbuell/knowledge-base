# Recovery & Sobriety Resource Library

The **Recovery & Sobriety Resource Library** is a connected collection of educational pages about addiction, beginning recovery, preventing relapse, addressing mental health, strengthening relationships, developing purpose, and finding support.

The library is primarily intended for people who are struggling with addiction, considering recovery, or maintaining sobriety. It may also help family members, friends, church leaders, and others who want to better understand the recovery process.

> Recovery can feel overwhelming when someone does not know where to begin. This library organizes the information into smaller topics so readers can find the next useful step.
