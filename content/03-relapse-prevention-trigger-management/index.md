# Relapse Prevention and Trigger Management

---

## Category Overview

Relapse prevention involves recognizing risk early and having a plan before a craving or difficult situation becomes overwhelming.

This category explains how triggers and cravings develop, how they can be managed, and how someone can respond responsibly if a relapse occurs.

> A craving or setback should be taken seriously, but it does not automatically erase all previous recovery progress.

## A Basic Relapse-Prevention Process

1. Recognize the trigger.
2. Pause before acting.
3. Move away from the risky situation.
4. Contact a supportive person.
5. Use a healthy coping strategy.
6. Seek additional treatment or support when needed.
7. Review the recovery plan after a setback.
