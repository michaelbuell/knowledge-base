# Recovery Foundations

---

## Category Overview

This category focuses on the early building blocks of recovery. It moves from understanding addiction toward creating a practical plan, establishing healthier daily habits, and involving trustworthy people in the process.

The pages are useful for someone considering recovery, beginning recovery, or reviewing the strength of an existing recovery plan.

---

## Pages in This Category

### 1. [[1. Building A Personal Recovery Plan|Building a Personal Recovery Plan]]

Explains how to identify reasons for recovery, build a support team, change risky environments, and prepare for difficult moments.

### 2. [[2. Healthy Habits That Support Recovery|Healthy Habits That Support Recovery]]

Discusses daily routines involving sleep, nutrition, physical activity, emotional care, meaningful responsibilities, and healthy connection.

### 3. [[3. Accountability and Goal Setting in Recovery|Accountability and Goal Setting in Recovery]]

Explains the role of honest check-ins, trustworthy accountability partners, realistic goals, and measuring progress over time.

---

## Key Ideas

Recovery foundations may include:

- A written plan
- Professional treatment when needed
- Safe and supportive relationships
- Consistent routines
- Realistic short- and long-term goals
- Honest accountability
- A response plan for cravings and setbacks
