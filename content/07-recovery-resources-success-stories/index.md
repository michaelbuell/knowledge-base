# Recovery Resources & Success Stories

---

## Category Overview

This category serves as the practical resource section of the knowledge base. It includes places to search for treatment, different recovery programs, useful books and digital tools, crisis contacts, and examples of long-term recovery.

Programs, websites, apps, phone numbers, and services may change. Resource information should therefore be reviewed periodically.

## Crisis Reminder

> [!danger] Immediate Danger  
> Call **911** during an immediate life-threatening emergency.

> [!important] Crisis Support  
> In the United States, call or text **988** for suicide, mental health, or substance-use crisis support. Veterans and service members may call **988** and then press **1**.
